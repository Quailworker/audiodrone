HOW TO READ THIS

The watch replays simulated drone incidents along the UAE border, as our detection system saw them. It plays by itself and flies to each incident as it happens.

  • Border line (UAE on one side)
  • Microphone cluster: 2 rows along the border
  • Cluster flashing green: it hears a drone
  • Bearing: the direction a cluster hears it from
  • Tracked drone. The ring shows the position uncertainty, the line ahead is where it's heading
  • SIM TRUTH: where the drone really was (off by default)
  • Shelter · Patrol or garrison (site data from Zetaris)

Drag: move · Right-drag: rotate · Scroll: zoom · Esc: back to the whole border · Space: pause · N: next event
